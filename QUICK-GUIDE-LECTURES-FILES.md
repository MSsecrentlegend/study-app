# Quick Guide: Videos and Files

## Videos

1. Sign in with an account that has platform administrator access.
2. Open **Videos** and choose **Add video**.
3. Enter a title, subject, and YouTube link, then save.

Learners see the YouTube thumbnail with the lesson title and can watch it in a modal player.

## Study files

1. Sign in with an account that has platform administrator access.
2. Open **Files** and choose **Add file**.
3. Enter a title and subject, choose a PDF, text, Markdown, or image file, and upload it.

Learners can preview supported files or download them. Shared files are stored in the private Supabase bucket configured by the database schema.

For account setup and administrator access, see [Backend Setup](./BACKEND-SETUP.md) and [Shared Content Management](./MANAGEMENT.md).
